## Problem
Current constructive steering has high logit lift but poor decision-level control tradeoff:
- linear methods: 0% STEER with varying BREAK
- objective-aware runtime: 100% STEER on small split but high BREAK (66.7%)

Goal: reduce collateral breakage while preserving steer rate.

## Constraints
- Local Apple Silicon Mac (MPS)
- Open western models only (<=9B)
- No weight fine-tuning
- Inference-time interventions only
- Sequential runs to avoid MPS thrashing

## Proposed Wedge (smallest reversible scope)
1. Increase sample size for Block B to n>=30 (train/dev/test split)
2. Tighten capability guard (`max_control_drop` lower than current 0.34)
3. Keep objective-aware intervention and tune for composite score:
   score = STEER - break_penalty*BREAK - control_penalty*control_drop + logit_weight*logit_lift
4. Re-export benchmark table with CIs and random baseline

## Alternative Approaches
A) Aggressive optimization (more steps, higher norm budget): likely higher STEER but higher BREAK.
B) Conservative optimization (lower norm budget + stronger control guard): lower BREAK but risks STEER drop.
C) Two-stage objective (target margin first, then break minimization): best balance, modest complexity.

## Recommendation
Proceed with B first (reversible and safer), then C if B underperforms.

## Success Criteria
- Maintain non-trivial STEER (>30%) on larger split
- Reduce BREAK from 66.7% toward <=35%
- Control drop <=15%
- Updated `RESULTS.md` table with CIs and random baseline comparison
